## Short description (≤ 80 chars)

Find Ukrainian specialists and offer your services: a trust-based community

## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)

Sviy dlya Svoikh ("One of Our Own") is a Ukrainian platform that connects entrepreneurs and consumers based on trust and shared values.

We built a place where the Ukrainian diaspora and the wider community help each other run businesses and find services. We support business within our community.

What you can do:
• Find specialists among Ukrainians — by service, category and city
• Create a profile and describe your services
• Browse categories: home, auto, beauty, education, leisure and hobbies, kids, and more
• Send service requests, chat with providers and leave reviews

Why Sviy dlya Svoikh:
• Trust — profiles, ratings and reviews help you choose a reliable provider
• Support — every deal supports your own business, your family, your community
• Quality — the rating and review system helps keep service quality high

The app is free: no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The interface is available in Ukrainian and English (switch it in the menu or in your profile settings).

Join the community — create a profile today!

## App access instructions for reviewers (App content → App access)

The public pages above work without an account. To review the logged-in features, create a dedicated reviewer account (use "Viewer" or "Regular profile" registration — it needs only an e-mail and a password, no phone verification) and enter its credentials in Play Console → App access. Never commit the credentials to Git (`materials/google_play/test_users_credentials.csv` is git-ignored).

Suggested instructions text:

> Open the app. Tap "Log in" and sign in with the provided e-mail and password. The language can be switched at any time with the language button in the menu (Українська / English). Main features: Catalog (search specialists), Services, Requests, Chat, Favorites, Profile → Edit. Account deletion: open the menu → "Account deletion" (or https://sviydlyasvoih.com.ua/en/account-deletion) while signed in.
