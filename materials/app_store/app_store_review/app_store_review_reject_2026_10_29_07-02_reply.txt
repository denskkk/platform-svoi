2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Sviy dlya Svoikh ("One of Our Own for Our Own") is a free Ukrainian community platform connecting entrepreneurs and consumers on the basis of trust and shared values.
Problem: Ukrainians at home and abroad find it hard to locate trusted service providers and small businesses in their own community and language, and entrepreneurs have no dedicated place to present their services.
Value: a searchable directory of people, businesses and services (by service, profession, category and city); public profiles; public help requests; in-app messaging; reviews and ratings.
Audience: Ukrainian-speaking people and entrepreneurs in Ukraine and abroad. The interface is in Ukrainian and English.
