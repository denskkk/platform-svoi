2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
------------------------------------------------------------
Sviy dlya Svoikh ("One of Our Own for Our Own") is a free Ukrainian community platform that connects entrepreneurs and consumers on the basis of trust and shared values.

Problem it solves: Ukrainians at home and abroad (the diaspora) find it hard to locate trusted service providers and small businesses within their own community, especially in a new country or city and in their own language. Entrepreneurs in the community have no dedicated place to present their services to people who want to support them.

Value it provides:
- A searchable directory of people, businesses and services. Users search by service, profession, category and city.
- Public profiles for entrepreneurs, with their services, contacts and location.
- Public requests ("help requests"), where users post what they need and others respond.
- In-app messaging between users.
- Reviews and ratings that help keep service quality high.
- Categories such as home, auto, beauty, education, entertainment and hobbies, and everything for children.

Target audience: Ukrainian-speaking people and Ukrainian entrepreneurs in Ukraine and abroad, and anyone who wants to find or offer services within this community. The interface is available in Ukrainian and English (language switch in the navigation menu).

Steps:
1. Launch the app. The home screen is shown, with "Create a profile" and "Join as a viewer" at the bottom.
2. Open the menu (top right), tap "Sign in", and enter the demo credentials. Alternatively, tap "Join as a viewer" to browse without a full profile.

   - Home
   - Search (People Directory: search by name or profession, filter by city)

5. To create content: Profile > "Add" under Services, or create a help request from the profile page.
6. To create a new account: tap "Create a profile" and choose Standard, Business or Viewer. Registration requires acceptance of the Terms of Use.
7. To delete the account: Profile > account deletion page (also at https://sviydlyasvoih.com.ua/account-deletion). Enter the password, tick the confirmation box, and tap "Delete account permanently". Account deletion is available in the app.
